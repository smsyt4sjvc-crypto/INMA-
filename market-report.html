Spokane Market Report

Why this report exists. Most homeowners are told to get three to five estimates. What they actually get is three to five numbers that vary by 50–100%, written in different languages, with different scopes, and no way to compare them honestly. We bring the market to you — the ranges below come from current Spokane-area cost data, national consumer guides, and real contract pricing from national firms we've reviewed. Read through it while we scope your project. By the time we present your estimate, you'll already know where it should land.

Real Spokane contract pricing from national firms we've reviewed: ~$29/SF installed on full-scope Hardie jobs, siding only — no soffit or fascia included.

The spread: Public guides say $10–$15/SF for primed, $16–$22/SF for ColorPlus. National firms run $25–$30+/SF. That gap is corporate overhead, national ad spend, and sales commissions — not the work itself.

What Spokane neighbors reported, October 2026: a DaBella quote of $150,000 for siding plus windows in Morgan Acres. A local Spokane contractor came in at half that number for the same job. Neighbor-reported on Nextdoor, not a verified invoice.

Final pricing is job-dependent based on actual scope and material selection. Contractor and turn-key figures include tear-off, wrap, trim, soffit, and fascia; national guides often price the siding alone.
