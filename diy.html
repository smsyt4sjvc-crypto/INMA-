DIY Assistance

25 years of exterior remodeling expertise in your corner — without the full project cost.

Siding · Windows · Doors · Paint

I'm not a contractor. I'm your personal homeowner advocate. On a DIY job that means the same thing it always does: I work for you, not the crew doing the work — and this time the crew is you.

Why hire me

Do it yourself — without flying blind.

Tackling your own exterior project saves real money. It also costs real money when a wrong cut, wrong fastener, or wrong product choice sends you back to the store three times — or forces you to tear something out. I've been doing this in Spokane for a quarter century. For the price of a decent dinner out, you get my experience riding shotgun on your build.

How it works

Three steps. You're in the driver's seat.

House visit

I come out, walk the project, take measurements, and we agree on scope. You get a complete material takeoff and a written plan.

You start building

Order materials (or have me pick them up). Work at your pace. Text me photos and questions any time during the job.

Finish strong

I'll do a final walkthrough on request, point out any cleanup details, and make sure your finished work looks like it came from a pro.

Common questions

FAQ

What kinds of projects do you help with?

Siding (Hardie, LP, vinyl, wood), window replacement, exterior doors, trim, fascia, soffit, exterior painting. Basically anything on the outside of your house. If it's something I'd normally build, I can coach you through it.

How fast can you get me a house visit?

Usually within the same week. Call or text me — I'll tell you straight.

What if I start and realize I don't want to finish it?

No problem. I can either finish the job myself or bring in one of my vetted contractors to take over. You don't lose the money you've already spent on materials — we just pick up where you left off.

Do I need special tools?

For most exterior work: a good circular saw, a decent drill, a level, a chop saw, and a ladder or scaffold. I'll tell you if your project needs anything specialty (like a siding nailer) and whether to rent or buy.

What if I only need help for a few days?

The weekly support fee is pay-as-you-go — cancel any time. Only going to be at it one weekend? Pay one week. Stretching the job over a month? Pay by the week as you go.

What's your service area?

Spokane, Spokane Valley, Liberty Lake, Cheney, Mead, Post Falls, and Coeur d'Alene. Outside that area, add mileage.

Do you sell the materials?

No. I route you to the right supplier — either I pick them up for you (at cost + handling) or you pick them up yourself. I make nothing on the materials, so there's no incentive to upsell you.

Ready to start?

Text a photo of your project. I'll tell you if DIY makes sense for it — straight up, no pressure.
