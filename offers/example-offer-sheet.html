Scope of Work & Notes

Scope: Remove existing T1-11 and lap siding to sheathing on all four elevations (approx. 18 squares of wall). Inspect sheathing; replace rot found with 7/16 OSB (allowance of 4 sheets included below). Install Tyvek HomeWrap, flash all openings with self-adhered flashing, install 5/4x4 HardieTrim at corners and 5/4x4 at windows and doors with Z-flashing over all head trim. Install HardiePlank 8.25 ColorPlus lap siding, 7" exposure, blind nailed. Install aluminum soffit and 6" fascia wrap at all eaves. Caulk all butt joints and trim with ColorPlus-matched sealant. Haul away all debris. Site left broom clean.

Materials: James Hardie HardiePlank 8.25 ColorPlus, Arctic White. HardieTrim 5/4 ColorPlus. Tyvek HomeWrap. Aluminum soffit and fascia, white. Fastened per James Hardie best-practice guide. Manufacturer: 30-year non-prorated substrate warranty, 15-year ColorPlus finish warranty.

Exclusions: Window and door replacement. Electrical disconnects or reconnects. Painting of any non-ColorPlus surface. Permits (see note under the total).
