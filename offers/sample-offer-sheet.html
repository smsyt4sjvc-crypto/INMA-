My installation terms

Install to James Hardie's written HZ5 installation instructions so the factory warranties hold. That includes:

Gaps

  • Keep siding 6" above finished grade.
  • Keep siding 2" above roofs, paths, steps, and patios.
  • Leave a ¼" open gap above all flashing. Don't caulk it. On ColorPlus, paint the exposed edge with James Hardie touch-up.

Caulking

  • Leave a ⅛" gap wherever trim meets siding, window frames, doors, or any other material, and fill it with caulk. The one exception is existing soffit that stays: run trim tight to it with no caulk (scope item 9).
  • Leave a ⅛" gap wherever siding meets anything other than another siding board, and fill it with caulk.
  • Use factory edges only at butt joints between siding boards, in moderate contact, not caulked.
  • Seal all field-cut edges. On ColorPlus, use James Hardie touch-up or edge coater only.
  • Don't caulk nail heads on ColorPlus siding.

Trim and flashing

  • Don't let siding hang below trim.
  • Install metal flashing over every window, door, and horizontal trim board.
  • Install kickout flashing wherever a roof edge meets a wall.
  • Install flashing behind every butt joint.

You'll correct any deviation from these terms, applicable building codes, or James Hardie's installation instructions at your expense before final payment. I'll give you any deviations found at final inspection in writing. If they aren't corrected within 30 days of that notice, I'll hire another contractor to correct them and pay that cost from the remaining balance.

Scope of work

  1. Tear-off. Remove the existing board-and-batten siding and dispose of it off site. Re-side in lap siding with shake gables, as specified.
  2. Sheathing. Inspect the sheathing once it's exposed. Replace rotted sheathing at $100 per sheet ($3.12 per sq ft), labor and material. Send me photos of all damaged sheathing before you replace it.
  3. Housewrap. Install HardieWrap weather barrier per James Hardie best practices.
  4. Window trim, Tradesman style.
    • 6" head, flashed per my installation terms.
    • 4" sides.
    • Run the head 1" past each side trim.
    • Miter side trim at the bottom corners.
    • Leave all trim gaps ⅛" and caulk them, per my installation terms.
  5. Recessed windows, trim lining the opening.
    • Flash the sill to the exterior with flashing tape or metal so water drains out, per IRC R703.4, item 1 (window and door openings).
    • Wood windows: caulk trim to the window frame.
    • Aluminum or vinyl windows: run sill trim and sill flashing past the window face, beyond the weep holes. Don't butt trim or flashing against the window face or block the weep holes.
  6. Corners. HardieTrim corners, ColorPlus Arctic White. Set the bottom of each corner flush with or below the bottom of the siding.
  7. Trim fastening.
    • Fasten HardieTrim with 15-gauge finish nails.
    • Depth-adjust nail guns so heads sit flush, not driven into the face.
    • Primed trim: caulk nail holes.
    • ColorPlus trim: finish nail holes with James Hardie touch-up only.
  8. Gables.
    • Install shakes over a 10" belly band, flashed per my installation terms.
    • Shake start: set a 1" starter strip over the belly band flashing, leaving the ¼" gap. Set a second starter over it that extends 1" past the top of the shake's factory slots. Run the first course of shakes over both.
    • Fasten and caulk shakes by the same rules as trim and siding.
  9. Frieze.
    • Install 1½" frieze board at all soffits, tight to the soffit.
    • Existing soffit: don't caulk the frieze to it. The caulk won't match the soffit color.
    • New soffit: caulk the frieze to it.
    • In both cases, caulk where the new siding meets the frieze.

Installation Guidance
Offer INMA-100 · James Hardie HZ5

Install per James Hardie HZ5 written instructions. This page summarizes them. Where the terms or scope of this offer are more specific, the offer governs.

General requirements

  • Water-resistive barrier behind all siding and trim, installed with penetration and junction flashing per code. Siding goes over studs at 24" o.c. max, or direct to 7/16" OSB minimum.
  • Store material flat, dry, and covered. Never install wet or saturated planks (it causes shrinkage at butt joints). Carry planks and trim on edge.
  • Caulk, paint, or prime all field-cut edges. Primed product must be painted within 180 days, 100% acrylic topcoat only. No stain, no oil or alkyd paint.
  • Nothing installed where it can sit in standing water.

Gapping

  • Butt joints: planks in moderate contact, no gap, every butt joint on a stud. Stagger joints so they don't stack course over course or land directly above or below windows or above doors.
  • Siding to trim: minimum ⅛" gap, then caulk.
  • Above any horizontal flashing: minimum ¼" gap, left uncaulked so water can drain.
  • Frieze: run tight to the soffit, per scope item 9.

Caulking

  • Sealant: elastomeric meeting ASTM C920 Grade NS Class 25 or higher, or latex meeting ASTM C834, applied per the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
  • Caulk the ⅛" siding-to-trim gaps and trim joints. Don't caulk the ¼" drainage gap above flashing or the top edge of trim under its flashing.
  • ColorPlus: butt joints are flashed, not caulked (caulk weathers differently than the finish). Never caulk nail heads. Use ColorPlus touch-up and edge coater on cut edges.

Attachment: siding

  • Blind nail on the factory nail line (¾" to 1" from the top of the plank), ⅜" in from plank ends, into every stud, 24" o.c. max. Minimum 1¼" overlap between courses.
  • Blind nails, wood framing: siding nail 0.090" shank × 2", or 11 ga. roofing nail 1¼". Face nail only where code requires it for wind, never mixed with blind nailing on the same wall.
  • Starter strip 1¼" under the first course. First course set level and dropped ¼" below the starter edge.
  • All fasteners corrosion resistant: hot-dipped galvanized recommended, stainless near water. Drive perpendicular, heads snug and flush. No aluminum fasteners, clipped-head nails, or staples. Over-driven blind nails get hammered flush; over-driven face nails get countersunk and filled.

Attachment: trim

  • Trim goes up before siding around windows, doors, and openings. Siding butts into trim; never trim over lap siding.
  • Face nail with 2" minimum 16 ga. finish nails (this offer specifies 15 ga., scope item 7), stainless recommended. No closer than ½" from edges and 1" from ends, 16" o.c. max. Two nails every 16" on typical window trim and band boards, three on 8" trim, per the Hardie fastener table.
  • Fascia over 2x sub-fascia: 2" 16 ga. finish nails, 16" o.c. max. Direct to rafter tails: 8d siding nails minimum, never finish nails. Gutters never hang off trim; hangers go into rafter tails or roof sheathing.
  • Concealed fastening: HardieTrim Flat and Corner Tabs are the only approved hidden-fastener system (tabs stapled to the trim back with 18 ga. crown staples, then 6d siding nails through the tabs, 16" o.c. max). Not allowed on fascia, rake, or frieze.

Flashing and clearances

  • Joint flashing behind every butt joint (James Hardie's recommended joint treatment).
  • Z-flashing over all horizontal trim, including window and door heads and band boards.
  • Kickout flashing where a roof line ends at a wall, minimum 4" × 4" per IRC, with step flashing up the roof-to-wall joint.
  • Clearances: 6" above finished grade; 2" above roofs, decks, slabs, paths, and steps. Grade slopes away from the house (about 6" of fall in the first 10'). Penetration blocks (vents, pipes, hose bibs) flashed with Z-flashing and a ¼" uncaulked gap.

Source: James Hardie HZ5 instructions, HardiePlank HS11117 and HardieTrim TR1510 (Sept 2025). James Hardie ties warranty coverage to following its written instructions. Most recent versions: jameshardiepros.com.
