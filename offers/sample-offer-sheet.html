Installation terms of this offer

Siding to be installed per James Hardie's written HZ5 installation instructions to preserve factory warranties, including:

Gaps

  • Siding to be held 6" above finished grade.
  • Siding to be held 2" above roofs, paths, steps, and patios.
  • A ¼" open gap to be left above all flashing. This gap is not to be caulked. On ColorPlus, the exposed edge to be field painted with James Hardie touch-up.

Caulking

  • A ⅛" gap to be left wherever trim meets siding, window frames, doors, or any other material, and filled with caulk. Existing soffit that remains is the one exception: trim runs tight to it with no caulk, per the frieze terms in scope item 9.
  • A ⅛" gap to be left wherever siding meets anything other than another siding board, and filled with caulk.
  • Butt joints between siding boards to use factory edges only, in moderate contact, not caulked.
  • All field-cut edges to be sealed. On ColorPlus, with James Hardie touch-up or edge coater only.
  • On ColorPlus pre-finished siding, nail heads are not to be caulked.

Trim and flashing

  • Siding is not to hang below trim.
  • Metal flashing to be installed over every window, door, and horizontal trim board.
  • Kickout flashing to be installed wherever a roof edge meets a wall.
  • Flashing to be installed behind every butt joint.

Any deviation from the terms of this offer, applicable building codes, or James Hardie installation instructions is to be corrected at the contractor's expense before final payment. Deviations found at final inspection are to be given to the contractor in writing. If they are not corrected within 30 days of that notice, a third-party contractor will be retained to correct them, and the cost will be paid from the remaining balance.

Full James Hardie installation instructions: jameshardiepros.com (HZ5 HardiePlank lap siding). James Hardie directs installers there for the most recent version.

Scope of work

  1. Tear-off. Existing board-and-batten siding to be removed and disposed of off site. The house is to be re-sided in lap siding with shake gables, as specified.
  2. Sheathing. Sheathing to be visually inspected once exposed. Rotted sheathing to be replaced at $100 per sheet ($3.12 per sq ft), labor and material. Photos of all damaged sheathing to be provided to the homeowner before replacement.
  3. Housewrap. HardieWrap weather barrier to be installed per James Hardie best practices.
  4. Window trim, Tradesman style.
    • 6" head, flashed per the installation terms.
    • 4" sides.
    • Head to extend 1" past each side trim.
    • Side trim mitered at the bottom corners.
    • All trim gaps ⅛" and caulked, per the installation terms.
  5. Recessed windows, trim lining the opening.
    • Sill to be flashed to the exterior with flashing tape or metal, so water drains out, per IRC R703.4, item 1 (window and door openings).
    • Wood windows: trim to be caulked to the window frame.
    • Aluminum or vinyl windows: sill trim and sill flashing to extend past the window face, beyond the weep holes. Trim and flashing are not to butt against the window face or block the weep holes.
  6. Corners. HardieTrim corners, ColorPlus Arctic White. The bottom of each corner sits flush with or below the bottom of the siding.
  7. Trim fastening.
    • HardieTrim fastened with 15-gauge finish nails.
    • Nail guns to be depth-adjusted so heads sit flush, not driven into the face.
    • Primed trim: nail holes caulked.
    • ColorPlus trim: nail holes finished with James Hardie touch-up only.
  8. Gables.
    • Shakes over a 10" belly band, flashed per the installation terms.
    • Shake start: a 1" starter strip set over the belly band flashing, leaving the ¼" gap. A second starter over it that extends 1" past the top of the shake's factory slots. The first course of shakes goes over both.
    • Shake fastening and caulking to follow the same rules as trim and siding.
  9. Frieze.
    • 1½" frieze board at all soffits, run tight to the soffit.
    • Existing soffit: frieze not to be caulked to it, since the caulk won't match the soffit color.
    • New soffit: frieze to be caulked to it.
    • In both cases, caulk where the new siding meets the frieze.

How this price was set

Ask an AI or a cost guide what it costs to re-side a Spokane home in James Hardie ColorPlus, and you'll hear $10 to $21+ per square foot installed. It will also tell you the final number depends on trim, window count, gable complexity, flashing, and tear-off. It can't tell you what those come to on your house.

This offer can. It's priced at the midpoint of Spokane fair market for ColorPlus installed with tear-off, $19.00 per square foot, for a home of moderate complexity. Shake gables are $23.00, because they take more cuts and slower courses. The scope spells out every trim detail, flashing point, and gap that the online range leaves as "it depends."

* Contractor may add permitting costs and any applicable sales tax to the final invoice with attached receipts. This is standard practice, as each job's final costs are based on valuations.

Additional siding is billed at $19.00 per square foot and additional shake at $23.00 per square foot, labor and material. Additional trim is billed at three times the material cost per linear foot, installed. No added charges for pickups, deliveries, or extra trips. Contractor is to verify all quantities before ordering. Any change in price requires a written change order, approved in writing by the homeowner by email, text, or signature.

Payment terms

Upon acceptance of this offer, one down payment of up to 50% of the offer total will be made to cover materials and operating costs. No further payment will be made until the work is complete and accepted by the homeowner.

Once work has started, any absence from the job of more than 3 consecutive working days without written notice to the homeowner (email or text) entitles the homeowner to end this agreement and retain another contractor. No further payment is owed to the original contractor, except any portion of the offer total left over after the replacement contractor completes the work and is paid in full.
