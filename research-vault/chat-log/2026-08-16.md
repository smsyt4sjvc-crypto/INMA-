## ⛔ CORRECTIONS MADE TODAY
<!-- with the CAUSE, so the pattern is visible across days rather than per-incident -->
- **⛔⛔ FOUR EPS-CONSTRUCTION BUGS in `durable_value_screen.py`. The 8/15 run was UNREPORTABLE** — it
  produced a complete, plausible, wrong result set. Caught only by hand-reading ten numbers.
  **A. Quarterly facts wearing annual clothes.** Valero tags its quarterly-EPS footnote `form="10-K"`.
  Keying on the `end` date alone let **Q4-2019 (2.58) overwrite FY-2019 (5.84)** — same end date, same
  form, same filing. VLO's "annual EPS history" was **five consecutive QUARTERS posing as five YEARS.**
  Chevron's 10-K carries no quarterly facts, so Chevron looked clean. **⇒ THE BUG WAS COMPANY-DEPENDENT.
  A one-name spot check could not have found it — and one-name spot checks are what I usually do.**
  **B.** Year-to-date summed with discrete quarters (a Q2 10-Q carries both; `fp` says "Q2" for both).
  **C. The structural one: Q4 discrete EPS is NEVER published in a 10-Q.** Summing four 10-Q quarters can
  never span a fiscal year-end. Not patchable by filtering forms.
  **D. The split bug, a SECOND time, inside the EPS history.** EDGAR restates comparatives only in filings
  made AFTER a split, so AAPL returned `[9.22, 8.31, 9.21, 2.98, 2.97, 3.28]` — three pre-split, three
  post-split. **The median of that list is what the durability ratio divides by.** AAPL durability
  0.88 → **1.94** once fixed, correctly reading 2021 as a spike.
- **⛔ THE CAUSE, and it is one cause for A/B/C: I TRUSTED THE LABEL OVER THE DATA.** `form`, `fp`, `fy`
  are metadata a filer chooses; `start`/`end` are the period itself. **Classify by the SPAN, never by the
  label.** Same shape as the `chartPreviousClose` error on 8/14 — a field that *reads* like the thing you
  want and is not it. **That is now twice in three days.**
- **⛔ A SILENT DROP I would never have seen:** the 3yr EPS CAGR raised a negative float to the 1/3 power.
  Python returns a **complex number**, `growth <= 0` then raised `TypeError`, and the caller's bare
  `except` turned it into `r = None`. **Every company with a negative latest annual EPS was being dropped
  by an exception, not by a filter.** Now guarded explicitly, and `main()` prints exceptions instead of
  swallowing them.
- **🗺️ MAP GAP #27 — the map had ZERO vocabulary for METHOD.** A paste describing a point-in-time EDGAR
