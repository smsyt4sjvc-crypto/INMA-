About "and up"

Starting prices cover a typical single-trade exterior job, like re-siding or windows on an average Spokane home. Larger homes, more trades, or more complex work cost more. You'll get your exact price before I start, and it doesn't change after you say yes.

What this isn't

I'm not a contractor. I don't take a cut of your contract, and I don't get paid by the crew. You pay me for the Offer Sheet and the review, and that's it.

INMA is not a contractor and does not hire, direct, or supervise any work. INMA provides project scoping, fair-market pricing, and homeowner advocacy. An Offer Sheet is an estimate of fair-market cost for the scope described, not a bid. Prices effective November 2026.
