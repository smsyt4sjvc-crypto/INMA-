## THESIS (interpretation — Jake's, NOT fact)
**AI is a behavioral transmission mechanism into market microstructure.** The asset class being
speculated on is simultaneously coaching its own investor base:
1. **Precedent on demand.** The frightened question — *"has this ever happened before?"* — now gets an
   instant, confident, historically-sampled answer. Fear of the unknown was most of fear; the unknown
   is gone.
2. **Verification + control at zero latency.** Pull from Meta, buy gold, back in a week; apps that buy
   the dip automatically. The gap between impulse and execution — where second thoughts lived — closed.
3. ⇒ **Structurally higher risk appetite for the MARGINAL investor**, concentrated into exactly the
   trillion-dollar names and mega-ETFs the precedent engine knows best.
4. ⇒ **The observed "resilience" and V-shaped recoveries are not mysterious.** They are the taught
   behavior executing at scale.

**Relationship to [[detachment-bid]]: two vectors, one bid.** Detachment = risk-seeking from
*hopelessness* (prospect-theory convexity below the reference point — "nothing to lose"). Precedent =
risk-appetite from *manufactured familiarity* — "nothing to fear." They compound: one removes the pain
of loss, the other removes the anticipation of it.
