   behavior executing at scale.

**Relationship to [[detachment-bid]]: two vectors, one bid.** Detachment = risk-seeking from
*hopelessness* (prospect-theory convexity below the reference point — "nothing to lose"). Precedent =
risk-appetite from *manufactured familiarity* — "nothing to fear." They compound: one removes the pain
of loss, the other removes the anticipation of it.
