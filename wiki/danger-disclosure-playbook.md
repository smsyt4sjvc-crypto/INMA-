## THESIS (interpretation — NOT fact; analysis unless tagged)
- **★★★ ONE MOVE, THREE PAYOFFS — Jake's three hypotheses are complements, not rivals:**
  1. **CAPABILITY PROJECTION (dominant payoff).** Public benchmarks are commoditized
     ([[compression-thesis]]); **a danger claim is the last unfalsifiable capability claim** — the lab
     controls the sandbox, the scenario, and the description; rivals cannot replicate-and-deflate it.
     The selection effect is the tell: escape stories are only ever told about models marketed as
     frontier.
  2. **LIABILITY FRONT-RUNNING (real; explains the DISCLOSURE, not the AMPLIFICATION).** Duty-of-care
     record (tested/warned/mitigated) + socializing the risk class (industry-wide behavior ≠ your
     defect). Meta adds a securities texture: public-company material-risk disclosure. The system card
     serves liability; the press cycle serves #1.
  3. **GOVERNMENT PRESSURE — wrong naive, right refined: GATES, NOT BRAKES.** Nobody is lobbying for
     caps (anti-mandate clause; labs drafted the framework). Danger narratives justify
     **capability-keyed regimes with classified thresholds** — compliance moats incumbents absorb and
     small labs cannot. The regulation being invited has a membership list.
- **★★★ THE TIMING READ: an announcement of hacking capability, the week the cyber-keyed designation
  regime went live, is functionally an APPLICATION FOR COVERED STATUS** — public demonstration of the
  criterion, plus norm-setting while the voluntary framework is soft (first movers define "responsible
  frontier lab" at their own cost structure).
- **★★ WHY IT FEELS LIKE A RACE (Jake's word): a norm cascade with zero entry cost.** Once one lab
  discloses escapes, silence reads as incompetent evals or concealment. Triple payoff, no benchmark to
  fail, no recall — crowded fast.
- **★★ THE BORING BASE holds real weight:** institutionalized red-teaming (the EO formalizes it)
  mechanically produces findings. **The strategy lives in the amplification, not the finding.** Do not
  attribute to coordination what process explains — then grade the press release separately.
- **★★ META-SPECIFIC:** the audience may be INVESTORS before Washington — danger announcements as
  capex-narrative support for the one spender with no token revenue to point at.

## 🚩 THE GRADING RUBRIC (apply to each instance)
1. **Technical artifact attached** (methodology, named third-party red-teamer, reproducible setup) or
   press-only? Press-only ⇒ projection.
2. **Paired with advocacy** for the capability-keyed regime (comments, lobbying, the 8/4-meeting
   channel)? ⇒ the gate play.
3. **Lands on a funding/product/capex cycle?**
4. **THE FALSIFIER: costly restraint.** A lab delaying/degrading a model at real commercial cost over a
   danger finding = genuine safety act. Disclosure-plus-shipping-anyway = marketing. **Base rate of #4
   to date: nearly empty — that is the spine of the cynical read.**
